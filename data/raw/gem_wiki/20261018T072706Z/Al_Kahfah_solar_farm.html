Al Kahfah solar farm (محطة الكهفة للطاقة الشمسية) is an operating solar photovoltaic (PV) farm in Al Kahafah, Eastern Province, Saudi Arabia.

Project Details[edit | edit source]

Table 1: Phase-level project details for Al Kahfah solar farm[edit | edit source]

Status Commissioning year Nameplate capacity Technology Owner Operator
Operating[1] 2025[1] 1425 MWac[2] PV[2] ACWA Power Co [50%]; Water and Electricity Holding CO (Badeel) [49%][3] ACWA Power[4]
