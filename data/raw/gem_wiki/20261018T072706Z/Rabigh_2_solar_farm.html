Rabigh 2 solar farm (محطة رابغ ٢ للطاقة الشمسية) is a solar photovoltaic (PV) farm in pre-construction in Rabigh Industrial City, Makkah, Saudi Arabia.

Project Details[edit | edit source]

Table 1: Phase-level project details for Rabigh 2 solar farm[edit | edit source]

Status Commissioning year Nameplate capacity Technology Owner
Pre-construction[1] 2026 (planned)[2] 300 MWac[3] PV[3] Aljomaih Energy & Water Co Ltd; TotalEnergies SE[4]
