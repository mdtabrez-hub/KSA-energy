Other names: Yanbu IV Desalination Plant

Yanbu 4 Iwp solar farm (محطة ينبع للطاقة الشمسية) is an operating solar photovoltaic (PV) farm in Yanbu, Ar Rayyis, Saudi Arabia.

Project Details[edit | edit source]

Table 1: Phase-level project details for Yanbu 4 Iwp solar farm[edit | edit source]

Status Commissioning year Nameplate capacity Technology Owner Operator
Operating[1] 2024[1] 11 MWp/dc[1] PV[2] Mowah Co [30%]; ENGIE SA [40%]; Nesma Holding Co Ltd [30%][3] ENGIE[4]
