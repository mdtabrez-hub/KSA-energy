ISCC Waad Al Shamal CSP solar farm (محطة وعد الشمال للطاقة) is an operating solar thermal farm in Waad Al Shamal, Turaif, Saudi Arabia.

Project Details[edit | edit source]

Table 1: Phase-level project details for ISCC Waad Al Shamal CSP solar farm[edit | edit source]

Status Nameplate capacity Technology Owner Operator
Operating[1] 50 MWac[2] Solar Thermal[3] Saudi Electricity Co [100%] (الشركة السعودية للكهرباء)[2] General Electric[1]

More information on this solar thermal project can be found at csp.guru.
