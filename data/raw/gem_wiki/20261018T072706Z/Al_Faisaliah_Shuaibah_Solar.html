Other names: Faisaliah Solar Power Project

Al-Faisaliah (Shuaibah) Solar (محطة الشعيبة 1للطاقة الشمسية) is an operating solar photovoltaic (PV) farm in Al Faisaliah, Saudi Arabia.

Project Details[edit | edit source]

Table 1: Phase-level project details for Al-Faisaliah (Shuaibah) Solar[edit | edit source]

Status Commissioning year Nameplate capacity Technology Owner
Operating[1] 2024[2] 600 MWac[3] PV[4] Water and Electricity Holding CO (Badeel) [34%]; ACWA Power Co [35%]; Saudi Aramco Power Co [30%][5]
