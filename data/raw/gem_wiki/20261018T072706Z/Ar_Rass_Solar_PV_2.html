Other names: Ar Raas 2 solar farm

Ar Rass Solar PV 2 (محطة الرس٢ للطاقة الشمسية) is an operating solar photovoltaic (PV) farm in Ar Rass, Al Qassim Province, Saudi Arabia.

Project Details[edit | edit source]

Table 1: Phase-level project details for Ar Rass Solar PV 2[edit | edit source]

Status Commissioning year Nameplate capacity Technology Owner Operator
Operating[1] 2025[2] 2000 MWac[3] PV[4] ACWA Power Co [50%]; Water and Electricity Holding CO (Badeel) [49%][5] ACWA Power[3]
