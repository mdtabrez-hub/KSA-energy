ISCC Duba 1 solar farm (محطة ضباء الخضراء لتوليد الطاقة) is an operating solar thermal farm in Duba, Tabuk, Saudi Arabia.

Project Details[edit | edit source]

Table 1: Phase-level project details for ISCC Duba 1 solar farm[edit | edit source]

Status Commissioning year Nameplate capacity Technology Owner Operator
Operating[1] 2018[2] 43 MWac[3] Solar Thermal[4] Saudi Electricity Co [100%] (الشركة السعودية للكهرباء)[4] Electromechanical Works; Initec Energia[5]

More information on this solar thermal project can be found at csp.guru.
