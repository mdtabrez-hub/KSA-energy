Al Jubail-2 solar farm (محطة الجبيل ٢ للطاقة الشمسية) is a solar photovoltaic (PV) farm in pre-construction in Jubail, Eastern province, Saudi Arabia.

Project Details[edit | edit source]

Table 1: Phase-level project details for Al Jubail-2 solar farm[edit | edit source]

Status Nameplate capacity Technology Owner Operator
Pre-construction[1] 110 MW[2] PV[2] Saline Water Conversion Corp Corp [100%] (المؤسسة العامة لتحلية المياه المالحة)[2] Alfanar Group (شركة الفنار)[2]
