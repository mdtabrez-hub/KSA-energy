Other names: Laylaa PV IPP

Layla Solar PV Plant (محطة ليلى للطاقة الشمسية ١, محطة ليلى للطاقة الشمسية ٢) is an operating solar photovoltaic (PV) farm in Layla, Alaflaj, Riyadh, Saudi Arabia.

Project Details[edit | edit source]

Table 1: Phase-level project details for Layla Solar PV Plant[edit | edit source]

Phase name Status Commissioning year Nameplate capacity Technology Owner Operator
1 Operating[1] 2019[2] 10 MWac[3] PV[4] Saudi Electricity Co; Taqnia Energy; King Abdulaziz City for Science and Technology (الشركة السعودية للكهرباء; مدينة الملك عبدالعزيز للعلوم والتقنية)[5] –
2 Construction[6] 2027 (planned)[6] 80 MW[6] PV[7] Saudi Ministry of Energy [59%]; ACWA Power Co [40%][8] ACWA Power[7]
