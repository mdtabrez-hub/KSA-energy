Shuaibah 2 solar farm (محطة الشعيبة ٢ للطاقة الشمسية) is an operating solar photovoltaic (PV) farm in Al-Qassim Province, Saudi Arabia.

Project Details[edit | edit source]

Table 1: Phase-level project details for Shuaibah 2 solar farm[edit | edit source]

Status Commissioning year Nameplate capacity Technology Owner Operator
Operating[1] 2025[2] 2060 MWac[3] PV[4] Water and Electricity Holding CO (Badeel) [34%]; ACWA Power Co [35%]; Saudi Aramco Power Co [30%][5] ACWA Power[6]
