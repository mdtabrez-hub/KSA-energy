Other names: Rabigh Industrial Solar

Rabigh Solar PV (محطة رابغ ١ للطاقة الشمسية) is an operating solar photovoltaic (PV) farm in Rabigh Industrial City, Makkah, Saudi Arabia.

Table 1: Phase-level project details for Rabigh Solar PV[edit | edit source]

Status Commissioning year Nameplate capacity Technology Owner Operator
Operating[1] 2023[2] 400 MWp/dc[3] PV[4] Aljomaih Energy & Water Co Ltd; Marubeni Corp (丸紅株式会社)[5] China Energy Engineering (中国能源建设股份有限公司)[6]
