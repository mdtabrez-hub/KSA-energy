Al Masa'a solar farm (محطة المصع للطاقة الشمسية) is a solar photovoltaic (PV) farm in pre-construction in Hail, Saudi Arabia.

Status Commissioning year Nameplate capacity Technology Owner
Pre-construction[1] 2027 (planned)[2] 1000 MWac[3] PV[4] EDF Renewables; SPIC Huanghe Hydropower Development Co Ltd (法國電力再生能源公司)[5]
