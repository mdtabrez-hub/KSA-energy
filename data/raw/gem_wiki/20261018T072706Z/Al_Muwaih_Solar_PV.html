Al-Muwaih Solar PV (محطة الموية للطاقة الشمسية) is a solar photovoltaic (PV) farm under construction in Mecca, Saudi Arabia.

Project Details[edit | edit source]

Table 1: Phase-level project details for Al-Muwaih Solar PV[edit | edit source]

Status Commissioning year Nameplate capacity Technology Owner
Construction[1] 2027 (planned)[2] 2000 MW[3] PV[4] Saudi Aramco Power Co; Public Investment Fund (Saudi Arabia); ACWA Power Co (صندوق الاستثمارات العامه)[5]
